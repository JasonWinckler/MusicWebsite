05Good to know

Anything on your mind?

What kinds of event can I book you for?+

B2B events such as galas, company anniversaries and restaurant events, as well as private celebrations and weddings. Planning something different? Tell me about it.

Can singing and hosting be combined?+

Yes, you’re welcome to enquire about both. We’ll look at how singing and hosting can fit your programme.

Can I request particular songs or a performance language?+

Share your wishes as early as you can. We’ll discuss which songs and languages are possible, along with any preparation needed.

Where can you perform?+

Let me know where you’re planning your event. We’ll discuss the possibilities, travel and what’s needed at the venue.

What does a performance cost, and what equipment is needed?+

That depends on the type and length of the performance, the venue and the equipment. We’ll discuss fees, travel and technical details before you book.

Can I enquire before all the details are decided?+

Of course. A few sentences about your idea are enough to start. We’ll discuss availability and how we could work together.
