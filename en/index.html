05Before you get in touch

A few useful answers.

What kinds of event can I enquire about?+

You can enquire about live singing or hosting for corporate events, private celebrations and special occasions. The audience, atmosphere and programme matter most. Describe your idea and we can discuss whether my contribution is a good fit.

Can singing and hosting be combined?+

You are welcome to enquire about both. We can discuss a suitable combination based on your programme and practical arrangements.

Can I request particular songs or a performance language?+

Please share your preferences early. Repertoire, language and any preparation are discussed individually.

Where can you perform?+

I am based in Rheine, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany. Include the event location in your enquiry so we can discuss whether a performance there is possible and what arrangements it would involve.

What does a performance cost, and what equipment is needed?+

This depends on the contribution, location and programme. Fees, duration, travel and available or required equipment are discussed before an agreement.

Can I enquire before all the details are decided?+

Yes. A brief outline is enough to start the conversation. Let me know which details are still open. An enquiry does not confirm availability or a booking.
